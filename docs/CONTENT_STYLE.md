# Content style

Use friendly, concise British English. Lead with the outcome or next action. Prefer “organisation”, “customise” and “authorise”. Use sentence case.

Buttons use a verb and object: “Schedule post”, “Invite member”, “Reconnect account”. Avoid “OK”, “Submit” and unexplained jargon.

Errors state what happened, what was preserved and what to do next. Example: “Instagram did not accept this post. Your draft is safe. Reconnect the account, then try again.” Never blame the customer or expose provider payloads, tokens, stack traces or internal IDs.

Usage warnings include the measured limit and reset/upgrade path. Security messages identify the action, time and recovery path without including secrets. Do not claim encryption, compliance certification, retention periods, guaranteed publishing, platform partnership or AI-training behaviour unless the applicable evidence and policy are approved.
